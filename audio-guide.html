Gunan Stop 01 · 1:27 · Guzheng & Dizi Intro
Welcome to Gunan Street & Su Dongpo’s "Shushan"
古南街开场导览 · “紫砂源泉、大师摇篮”与苏东坡“此山似蜀”
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Hello everyone! Welcome to Shushan Ancient South Street, or Gunan Street. It is my great honor to accompany you today as we explore this historic neighborhood. First, please take a look at this map. We are currently standing at the entrance of Gunan Street, nestled between mountains and water. To our east is ancient Shushan Hill, where dragon kilns have climbed the slopes since the Song and Yuan dynasties, becoming the primary center for firing Zisha purple clay teapots in the Ming Dynasty. To our west flows the ancient Li River canal, providing convenient water transport. Firing kilns on the hill above and trading teaware at the foot below—that is the essence of Gunan Street, summed up in two phrases: "The Fountainhead of Zisha, and the Cradle of Masters." Now, let's step onto these ancient bluestone slabs and experience the charm of Gunan Street firsthand! Stretching over 900 meters, the street preserves a rich collection of Ming and Qing dynasty architecture. Everything here begins with Shushan Hill to the east. Originally called Dushan, or "Lonely Hill," because it stood alone on the flat plain, it got its current name when the great Song Dynasty poet Su Dongpo arrived here, bought farmland intending to retire, and exclaimed: "This hill looks just like my homeland of Shu in Sichuan!" Ever since, generations have called it Shushan.

02
Gunan Stop 02 · 0:58 · Guzheng Glissando
Small Square Kiln (Downdraft Kiln) & Hexin Factory
小方窑（倒烟窑）与原宜兴合新陶瓷厂历史
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

On our left is the "Downdraft Kiln," locally known as the "Small Square Kiln." This chimney belonged to the former Yixing Hexin Ceramic Factory. In a downdraft kiln, the smoke and flames circulate up and down inside the chamber for even heat transfer. It was simple to build, low-cost, and kept temperature differences very small. However, because it is an intermittent kiln with high heat loss and heavy emissions, its use is now restricted for environmental protection. The Yixing Hexin Ceramic Factory—originally founded in 1958 by merging 28 local Zisha workshops—was one of the key predecessors of the famous Yixing Zisha Craft Factory, employing over 3,000 artisans at its peak. In 1959, Grandmaster Gu Jingzhou was appointed deputy director of technical research here, helping design the Jiangsu Hall in Beijing's Great Hall of the People and crafting state tea sets exported worldwide.

Gunan Stop 03 · 1:01 · Water & Guzheng
Jinsha Pavilion (Gu Jingzhou Plaque) & "Shop in Front, Studio in Back"
金砂阁（顾景舟题匾、9300万拍卖纪录）与明清“前店后坊”格局
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Look up at the plaque above this doorway reading "Jinsha Pavilion." Those three characters were personally inscribed by the legendary Grandmaster of Zisha, Gu Jingzhou! A single teapot made by Gu Jingzhou holds the all-time auction record for Zisha ware at an astonishing 93 million RMB! Let's step inside for a look—rumor has it the family here still treasures a teapot by Gu Jingzhou. They are original residents of this street, where crafting teapots and sipping tea is simply everyday life. As you can see, the buildings along the entire street are two-story Ming and Qing wooden townhouses built between the hill and the canal. The ground floor features removable wooden shop planks, while the upper floor serves as living quarters with carved wooden lattice windows. Over half the families here craft and trade Zisha ware using the classic "Shop in Front, Workshop in Back" layout—selling teapots in the front room facing the street, and hand-building them in the back courtyard.

Gunan Stop 04 · 0:52 · Bamboo Flute
Shushan Gallery, Gu Shaopei Residence & Dragon Kiln Saggars
蜀山展示馆、陶园顾脉（顾绍培旧居）与龙窑烧制容器“匣钵（得管）”
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Right here is the Shushan Exhibition Hall, a community gallery that hosts rotating local art exhibitions. And just ahead is "Taoyuan Gumai," the former residence of Chinese Arts and Crafts Master Gu Shaopei. As we mentioned earlier, Gunan Street is truly the cradle of Zisha masters, and nearly every great master's story is intertwined with this lane. Please take a look at these round ceramic cylinders—we call them "Saggars," or "Deguan" in the local dialect. Artisans place raw Zisha teapots inside these protective clay containers before loading them into the Dragon Kiln. The saggar shields the delicate purple clay from direct open flames and flying wood ash, preventing surface blisters and uneven coloring. Today, authentic dragon-kiln saggars like these have become a rare sight.

Gunan Stop 05 · 0:47 · Guzheng Interlude
Master Ji Yishun Residence & Ming-Qing Commercial Hub
季益顺旧居与古南街“因商而成、因市而荣”的百业风貌
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Next, we pass the former residence of Master Ji Yishun, born right here on Gunan Street in 1960. Crowned both a Chinese Arts and Crafts Master and a Ceramic Art Master, his works are celebrated for their poetic themes and ingenious, lifelike forms. Protected as a Jiangsu Provincial Historic Preservation District, Gunan Street was once the bustling "Wall Street of Zisha." During the Ming and Qing dynasties, it served as Yixing's commercial and passenger transport hub. Beyond pottery workshops, the street was packed with grocery emporiums, tea houses, silk draperies, bamboo craft shops, clockmakers, and clinics—a vibrant, self-contained riverside town.

Gunan Stop 06 · 0:45 · Canal Water & Guzheng
Grandmaster Xu Hantang Residence & The Kiln-to-Canal Lane
徐汉棠旧居（顾老第一弟子、“汉棠盆”）与连通蜀山龙窑和蠡河的唯一古道
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Here stands the former residence of Grandmaster Xu Hantang, born in 1932 and honored with the Chinese Arts and Crafts Lifetime Achievement Award. In 1954, he became the very first disciple of Grandmaster Gu Jingzhou. Master Xu is renowned for his ice-crackle glaze textures and his exquisite miniature bonsai pots, celebrated by collectors as "Hantang Pots." Notice this narrow lane beside us: it was the sole passageway connecting the dragon kilns on Shushan Hill with the Li River canal. Freshly fired teapots were carried down this very path to waiting cargo boats on the Li River, embarking on their waterborne journey to tea lovers around the world.

Gunan Stop 07 · 0:29 · Breeze & Chime
Prosperous Corner & 1934 Shushan Agricultural Bank
繁华街角商号与民国23年（1934）蜀山农业银行旧址
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Rounding the corner, we enter what was once the most prosperous commercial section of Gunan Street, lined with pottery shops, general stores, teahouses, and bakeries. On our left stands the former site of the Shushan Agricultural Bank. Back in April 1934, the Yixing branch of the Jiangsu Farmers Bank established a granary warehouse right here, allowing local farmers and potters to store agricultural goods as collateral for loans.

Gunan Stop 08 · 0:46 · Classical Guzheng
Zisha Trade Guild & Five-Color Raw Mineral Ores
紫砂同业公会旧址、紫泥/红泥/绿泥原矿石与紫砂厂老照片展
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

We have now arrived at the former site of the Zisha Trade Guild—the historical equivalent of today's industry association. In its heyday, the guild played a vital role in regulating quality standards, stabilizing teapot prices, and promoting Yixing Zisha across China. Displayed right in front of us are the raw Zisha mineral ores—Purple Clay, Red Clay, and Green Clay. Dug from deep rock strata, these stone-like ores must undergo months of open-air weathering, crushing, screening, water mixing, and aging before they transform into the supple clay used to hand-build teapots. Inside the hall, you can also see historical photographs and artifacts from the 20th-century Zisha Craft Factory era.

Gunan Stop 09 · 1:02 · Guzheng & Dizi Finale
Century-Old Deyilou Teahouse & The "Seven Old Masters" Finale
百年老字号“得义楼”茶馆与1954年宜兴紫砂工艺厂“七大老艺人”传奇
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Finally, look at the century-old "Deyilou Teahouse" before us. Back in the 1920s, Shushan Town had barely 300 households and fewer than 2,000 residents—yet it boasted seven lively teahouses! Every morning, every single table was packed. Gathering here for morning tea wasn't just a daily habit; it was the town's social network where potters and merchants exchanged news and forged friendships. Before we conclude, one last piece of history: nearby stands the former site of the state-owned Yixing Zisha Craft Factory, founded at the end of 1954 by the legendary "Seven Old Masters of Zisha"—Wu Yungen, Pei Shimin, Ren Ganting, Wang Yinchun, Zhu Kexin, Gu Jingzhou, and Jiang Rong. Over four decades, it nurtured generations of national masters and shaped modern Zisha history. That concludes our guided walk through Shushan Gunan Street today—thank you all for joining!

Bamboo Sea Stop 01 · 1:46 · Guzheng & Dizi Intro
Welcome & Overview: "First Bamboo Sea in East China"
开场导览 · 苏浙皖三省交界、18万亩竹乡与“索道上山、徒步下山”行程总览
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Hello everyone! Welcome to the Yixing Bamboo Sea Scenic Area. It is my great honor to accompany you as we stroll through this vast bamboo forest, covering tens of thousands of acres, to experience the refreshing coolness and poetic beauty of what is known as the "First Bamboo Sea in East China." Before we officially head into the mountains, please take a look at this map for a general idea of today's itinerary. The Yixing Bamboo Sea is located in Hufu Town, about 31 kilometers southwest of downtown Yixing. It sits right at the junction of three provinces: Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Anhui. With hundreds of thousands of acres of lush bamboo connecting across these three provinces, it truly is an "ocean of bamboo." Because it is the largest bamboo resource scenic area in East China, it has earned that resounding title—the "First Bamboo Sea in East China." Our tour today will be very relaxing. We will first take the cable car up the mountain to get a bird's-eye view of the bamboo sea, and then we will hike all the way down to fully appreciate the beautiful scenery. Through this journey up and down, the joy of the mountains and the charm of the forests and springs will unfold before your eyes. Before we enter, let me briefly mention the deep connection between Yixing and bamboo. Yixing itself is a famous "bamboo town" and the most important producing area of Moso bamboo in Jiangsu Province, with 180,000 acres of bamboo forests citywide. In the southern mountainous area where we are now, there are about 12,000 hectares of Moso bamboo, making it the largest bamboo production base in the province. The bamboo sea before you is the absolute essence of this region.

02
Bamboo Sea Stop 02 · 1:53 · Water Drops & Guzheng
Mirror Lake & The "Water Panda" (Peach Blossom Jellyfish)
镜湖秀色、映竹轩与6.5亿年前的活化石“水中大熊猫”桃花水母
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Entering the scenic area and walking along the path, the first spot we encounter is Jing Lake, or Mirror Lake. Look over there—nestled between two mountains lies a tranquil lake. The water surface is as flat as a giant mirror, perfectly reflecting the blue sky, white clouds, green mountains, and emerald bamboo, which is exactly how it got its name. The lake covers an area of 30,000 square meters, holds 150,000 cubic meters of water, and reaches a depth of 10 meters. The water is crystal clear all year round. As the local saying goes, "Bamboo grows in the water, and water flows through the bamboo," creating a beautiful contrast between the lake and the forest. The pavilion by the lake is called Yingzhu Pavilion. If you stand here and look towards the mountains, the sparkling water, the emerald bamboo, and the blue sky come together like a rich, vibrant landscape painting. You might not expect this, but living beneath the surface of this lake is a group of incredible "ancestors." Jing Lake is home to a rare creature called the Peach Blossom Jellyfish. It first appeared 650 million years ago, hundreds of millions of years before the dinosaurs! It is a "living fossil" for evolutionary research and is often praised as the "Giant Panda of the Water." From August to November, thousands of these jellyfish gather in the water near the Jellyfish Pavilion on the east side of the lake. The larger ones are about the size of a coin, while most are the size of a contact lens. As they swim and pulse, they look like crystal-clear petals blooming in the water. The Peach Blossom Jellyfish requires extremely high water quality and cannot tolerate even the slightest pollution. The fact that they thrive here proves just how incredibly pure the water of Jing Lake is.

Cable Car Ride (1,500m Aerial Gondola)
索道凌空 · 全长1500米、落差400米，体验武侠轻功“凌波微步”掠过竹梢

Now that we've seen Jing Lake, it's time to take the cable car up the mountain. Please follow me to the left; we'll reach the boarding station in just a few minutes. As you can see, this cableway is 1,500 meters long with a vertical drop of 400 meters between the top and bottom stations. The mountains covered in bamboo will glide beneath you, with the bamboo tops rolling and surging in the wind. It feels as if we are martial arts masters from a movie, using "lightness skills" to fly swiftly over the bamboo canopy. It's a magical feeling you can only understand by experiencing it yourself. I highly recommend using these ten minutes in the air to take out your phones and shoot your very own aerial blockbuster of the bamboo sea.

Bamboo Sea Stop 04 · 2:26 · Summit Bamboo Flute
The Summit: Yunv Peak (611.5m · One Foot in Three Provinces)
苏南第一峰·玉女峰 · 登凌云阁一脚踏三省、冒龙池不涸清泉与树蛙
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

We've arrived at the top station. Please watch your step as you exit the cable car. We just need to climb a short flight of stairs to reach the highest point of the bamboo sea—Yunv Peak. Everyone, the place we are standing right now is the famous "Highest Peak in Southern Jiangsu." With an altitude of 611.5 meters, it sits exactly at the junction of Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Anhui provinces. It is the highest peak in this vast mountainous region. Once you've caught your breath, look far out in every direction. The mountains are deep and elegant, the breeze is refreshing, and the scenery of the three provinces is fully in view. Interestingly, if you look north, that's Yixing in Jiangsu; look west, and you see Xuancheng in Anhui; look south, and that's Huzhou in Zhejiang. It's not every day you get to stand with one foot in three different provinces! The pavilion here at the summit is called Lingyun Pavilion. If you climb up, you can enjoy a 360-degree panoramic view. On a clear day, you can even see the shimmering waters of Taihu Lake in the distance. Look around as the mountain wind blows—the endless bamboo forest rolls and surges just like the waves of the ocean, while Jing Lake down below looks like a piece of emerald quietly embedded in the forest. This is exactly where the name "Bamboo Sea" comes from, and it is truly its most moving moment. At the top of Yunv Peak, there is also a clear spring that never dries up all year round. Locals call it Maolong Pool. It is formed by spring water gathering from the underground mountain range. Because the bamboo sea is part of the Tianmu Mountain range, the vegetation is lush and water resources are abundant, forming a massive underground reservoir. Even during long droughts, the trees here still receive plenty of nutrients. Next to this clear spring lives a protected species—the tree frog. If we're lucky, we might just spot one today. Now that we've enjoyed the breeze and taken enough photos at the summit, we will begin the most relaxing part of today's journey: hiking down the mountain. As we descend, the sound of the streams will grow louder. We will be accompanied by the babbling spring water all the way down, feeling as if we are walking inside a painting. Please follow me, slow down your pace, and truly enjoy this time deep in the bamboo forest.

The air here has an extremely high concentration of negative oxygen ions. It is a true "natural oxygen bar," perfect for cleansing your lungs and leaving you feeling completely refreshed.

Bamboo Sea Stop 06 · 1:24 · Classical Guzheng
Emerald Corridor & Su Dongpo’s Love for Bamboo
翡翠长廊 · 苏东坡“宁可食无肉，不可居无竹”与花中四君子
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

After passing the suspended boardwalk, we arrive at the Emerald Corridor. Look at the tall, graceful bamboo on both sides—they are as green as emeralds. The bamboo branches above our heads intertwine to form a natural corridor. As sunlight filters through the leaves, it creates beautiful, dappled shadows on the ground. Walking through here, every step offers a new scenic view, and any photo you take will look like a classic masterpiece. Speaking of bamboo, let's take a moment to appreciate the special love Chinese people have for it. The great poet Su Dongpo from the Song Dynasty once wrote: "I would rather eat a meal without meat than live in a place without bamboo. A lack of meat makes one thin, but a lack of bamboo makes one vulgar." Su Dongpo had a deep connection with Yixing; he even bought land here with the intention of retiring. It was likely his deep affection for bamboo that made him linger among the mountains and waters of Yixing. Because bamboo is hollow inside but stands tall and straight, and grows tenaciously anywhere, it has always been viewed by Chinese people as a symbol of a gentleman's noble character. That's why bamboo is honored as one of the "Four Noble Men" of plants in traditional culture. By walking through this bamboo sea today, we can all consider ourselves "refined" people!

Bamboo Sea Stop 07 · 0:41 · Mountain Brook & Guzheng
The First Source of Taihu Lake (Bamboo Ravine Stream)
竹林溪涧·太湖第一源 · 富含锗、锂、硒、钙等微量元素的纯净山泉
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Continuing our way down, the mountain streams gradually grow larger. The Yixing Bamboo Sea is located in the Hufu mountainous area, which is the closest and most distinct watershed to Taihu Lake. The mountain springs flow through the ravines, babbling all the way down until they finally merge into Taihu Lake. Because of this, the stream flowing down from the bamboo sea has a very proud name: "The First Source of Taihu Lake." This spring flows endlessly year-round and the water is incredibly pure. Experts have tested it and found it contains various healthy minerals like germanium, lithium, selenium, and calcium.

Bamboo Sea Stop 08 · 0:42 · Water Drops & Guzheng
Sea Bottom & Swaying Suspension Bridge (索桥凌波·太湖之源)
山脚“海底”太湖之源刻石、源头活水池与横跨溪涧的索桥

We have now reached the foot of the mountain, known as the "Sea Bottom"—which simply means the bottom of the bamboo sea. The spring water from the mountain sets off from here and flows into Taihu Lake, hence the name. Standing here and touching the water from the very source of Taihu Lake is quite an elegant experience. To the west of the lake, there is also a swaying suspension bridge that spans across the stream. Walking on it with the water rushing beneath your feet is a lot of fun and adds a special charm to the trip.

Bamboo Sea Stop 09 · 0:44 · Zen Muyu & Bronze Bell
Jizhao Temple & The 1,000-Year-Old "Tree within a Tree"
寂照禅寺 · 千年古银杏与寄生朴树“树中树”奇观
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Finally, we arrive at Jizhao Temple, hidden deep within the bamboo forest. The temple sits beside ancient trees in a wonderfully serene environment. In front of the temple stands a thousand-year-old ginkgo tree. It is over 30 meters tall with a trunk circumference of 5 meters. What's even more fascinating is that right in the middle of this ancient ginkgo's branches grows a hackberry tree. It survives by drawing nutrients from the ginkgo, creating an absolute botanical marvel known as the "Tree within a Tree." Deep in the bamboo sea, listening to the temple bell and gazing at the ancient tree, our restless minds can finally find peace.

Bamboo Sea Stop 11 · 1:55 · Guzheng & Dizi Finale
Extra Facts: 140 Bamboo Species, Square Bamboo & Snake Lizard
其他相关知识点 · 宜兴竹园、凤尾竹/人面竹/四方竹/实心竹与濒危脆蛇蜥
Read Script / 查看英文讲解词

Let me also share some extra facts about the area before we part ways. Inside the scenic area is the Yixing Bamboo Garden, which has introduced over 140 species of bamboo, totaling 162,000 plants, along with various ornamental trees and plants. The garden features many beautifully designed pavilions and gathers a wide variety of unique and exotic bamboos, each with its own graceful posture. Among the rare bamboos, you can find the Phoenix-tail Bamboo, which looks beautifully like the tail feathers of a phoenix. There is the Human-face Bamboo, where the patterns on the trunk closely resemble human faces—some looking like old men and others like children, making it very rare and precious. We also have the Square Bamboo, which actually has a square trunk. Legend has it that Emperor Qianlong planted his chopsticks upside down during a tour, and they grew into this bamboo. Finally, there is the Solid Bamboo. While most bamboos are hollow, this rare type is completely solid inside, making it an excellent material for bamboo crafts. As for rare animals, besides the ancient Peach Blossom Jellyfish in Jing Lake that we mentioned earlier, the bamboo sea is also home to the Snake Lizard. This is a rare and endangered wild animal that looks like a snake because its legs have degenerated from living underground for so long. In Jiangsu Province, it can only be found right here in the Yixing mountains. The scenic area boasts many major attractions, such as the First Source of Taihu Lake, the Highest Peak in Southern Jiangsu, Jing Lake, Jizhao Temple, the Emerald Corridor, and the Suspended Boardwalk. Whether they are grand and majestic, quiet and profound, or winding and mysterious, each has its own unique charm. Thank you all for joining me today, and I hope you had a wonderful time!
