How to Make a Purple Clay Cup · Visual Guide

Unlike wheel-thrown porcelain, Yixing purple clay is prized for its sandy, double-porosity mineral structure and is shaped by beating clay slabs, wrapping the cylinder body, and joining with clay slip. Follow the 6 core stages below to craft and personalize your own cup.

Sizing Template Disc 5.0 mm Defines the cup diameter
Wall & Base Slab 3.0 – 3.5 mm Beaten flat and uniform
Seam & Base Joints 45° Bevel Cut Maximizes bonding area
Bonding Paste Clay Slip Seals the base & cylinder
Leak Prevention Plug Pinhole Seal center compass hole

    Essential Workshop Tools

    The 8 traditional tools used throughout the cup-making process:

    Wooden Mallet
    Beats clay blocks into flat, even slabs
    Compass Cutter
    Cuts precise circular clay discs
    Steel Ruler & Knife
    3-point marking & 45° bevel cutting
    Banding Wheel
    Smooth rotation for shaping & trimming
    Wooden Paddle
    Compacts base disc onto cylinder body
    Clay Slip & Slab
    Adhesive clay paste for joining the base
    Damp Chamois Cloth
    Polishes and rounds the drinking rim
    Stylus / Fine Brush
    Adds your unique personal marking
